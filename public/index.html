How it works

Up and running in seconds

No extensions, no downloads, no setup. Just a link and you're in together.

01
🔐
Create Your Account
Sign up with Google (one click) or pick a unique username and password. Your account stores all your created rooms.
02
🏠
Create a Room
Hit "Create Room" on your dashboard. Set a room name, max number of people, optional expiry time, and an optional password. You get a unique link and a QR code to share.
03
🔗
Share the Link
Copy the room link or share the QR code with your friends. They enter your token, pass the password if you set one, and they're in instantly.
04
🎬
Watch or Read Together
As the room creator, paste a streaming video URL or upload a PDF. Every play, pause, seek, and page flip is synced for everyone in real time. Chat and talk while you enjoy.
Features

Everything in one place

Built for anime fans, manga readers, movie nights, and study groups.

🎬
Video Sync
Paste any direct MP4, WebM, or HLS (.m3u8) streaming URL. Every play, pause, and seek is synced to the millisecond for everyone.
📖
PDF / Manga / Ebook Viewer
Upload PDFs or paste direct links. Switch between webtoon-style horizontal scroll or single-page vertical mode. All page turns are synced.
🎙️
Voice Chat
Peer-to-peer real-time voice chat. Mute yourself or locally mute others. Room creator can globally mute or unmute anyone in the room.
💬
Live Text Chat
A live chat panel for reactions and discussion. Messages appear instantly for everyone. React, hype, and vibe together.
🔒
Room Security & Control
Password-protect your room, set a user limit, and add an expiry date/time. The creator can kick members and control who hears what.
📱
QR Code Join
Share a QR code for instant joining. Friends can scan it with their camera or upload a QR image from their gallery.
📄
Multi-PDF Queue
Queue up multiple PDFs in sequence. Read manga volumes one after another without interruption — pages flow seamlessly.
👑
Creator Controls
Full control in the hands of the room creator. Load media, change content, add subtitles, kick & mute — others just enjoy.
