📡 How to Get Streaming Links

🎬 Video Streaming Link

Google Drive: Upload video → Share → "Anyone with link" → Copy ID from URL → Use a GDrive direct link generator → paste the .mp4 direct link here.
Mega.nz: Upload → right-click → "Get Link" → paste directly. Works as-is.
Streamtape / Doodstream / Filemoon: Upload → click "Share/Embed" → copy the direct .mp4 URL.
Direct URL: Any link ending in .mp4, .webm, or .m3u8 works directly!

📖 PDF Streaming Link

Google Drive: Upload PDF → Share → "Anyone with link" → Change /view to /preview in the URL, or use: drive.google.com/uc?export=download&id=FILE_ID
Direct URL: Any link ending in .pdf works directly! Example: https://example.com/book.pdf
Or just upload from your device directly — max 150MB, auto-deleted after 24 hours.
